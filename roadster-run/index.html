HOW TO FLY

  • Space↑ Press to jump. Keep holding after the top of a jump to hover on the thrusters.
  • ↓ Hold on the road to drop into suction mode, so drones pass over you. Hold it in the air to drop fast.
  • V Press to switch between the 2D screen and the three 3D cameras. You can switch in the middle of a run.
  • C Press to change the colors.
  • PM Press P to pause and M to turn the sound off.

Hovering uses air. You get air back slowly while you drive on the road. Each air canister you grab gives you half a tank.

On a phone, tap the screen to jump. Hold the left third of the screen or the SUCTION button to duck. You can also play with a game controller.

FROM THE OCT 15 DEMO

  • Each run starts with a standing lift-off and a 0 to 60 mph launch in 0.81 seconds.
  • The rows of parked cars are based on the Cybercab, Cybertruck, Semi, Model Y and Model 3 that the real car jumped over.
  • The flip ramps are based on the ramp that the real car drove up before it spun around and landed.
  • The ten air tanks stand for the 600 liters of compressed air that feed the car's 10 cold gas thrusters.
  • The canyon jumps are in the game because Musk said they may try to jump the Snake River Canyon.
